FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about products, quotations and project supply.

What pipes and fittings do you supply?

We supply PPRC, UPVC pressure, UPVC SCH-40, UPVC SCH-80, HDPE, CPVC, GI, MS, SS and sewerage and drainage piping products.

What type of valves do you supply?

Our valve range includes brass valves, gate valves, NRVs, Y-strainers, pressure reducing valves and flow-control products.

Yes. We handle project, bulk and regular material requirements depending on product, quantity and specification.

Yes. You can mention your preferred brand or manufacturer in the quotation request.

Who can request a quotation?
