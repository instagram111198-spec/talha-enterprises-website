Common questions about quotations, products and project requirements.

What building materials do you supply?

We supply PPRC, UPVC, HDPE, CPVC, GI, MS and SS pipes and fittings along with various valves and related piping products.

Can I request a quotation for a large project?

Yes. You can submit your company name, contact details, product, specification, quantity and project location through our quotation form.

Yes. The quotation form includes a Brand / Manufacturer field where you can specify your preferred brand.

Do you supply bulk quantities?

Yes. The website is designed to accept project, bulk and regular material requirements.

How can I request prices?

Use the quotation form or contact Talha Enterprises through WhatsApp to send your requirement and request pricing.

What information should I provide for a quotation?

Ideally provide the company name, contact person, WhatsApp number, location, brand, product, size, specification and quantity.
